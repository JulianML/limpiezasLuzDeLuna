Three decades of clarity and commitment in Benidorm

Back in 1996, Limpiezas Luz de Luna was born with a clear goal: to professionalise a trade that demands precision, height and trust. Today, 30 years on, we have established ourselves as the leading window cleaning service across the Marina Baixa, keeping the same passion for detail as on day one.

From a family business to leaders in technical cleaning

What started as a small local project in Benidorm has grown into a company capable of cleaning the area's most iconic skyscrapers and prestigious hotels.

Constant evolution

Over the years, we have watched our city's architecture evolve. We have moved from traditional ladder cleaning to pioneering the use of pure-water poles and technical rope-access systems.

Experience that translates into safety

We don't just clean glass — we manage risk. Our long track record has allowed us to refine safety protocols that guarantee absolute peace of mind for our clients.
