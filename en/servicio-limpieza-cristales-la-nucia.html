Solutions for La Nucía's residential architecture

La Nucía doesn't have Benidorm's salt spray, but it does face mountain dust, pollen and muddy rain. We adapt to the area's homes:

  • Enclosed terraces & glass curtains: Very common in local townhouses. We leave them spotless, also cleaning the tracks and profiles so they slide smoothly.
  • Villa picture windows: We reach those fixed or high-rise glass panels typical of modern living rooms in developments like Bello Horizonte or Panorama.
  • Solar panel cleaning: While we're there, we clean your photovoltaic panels with pure water so they recover maximum energy efficiency.

Technology that respects your garden and façades

In La Nucía's villas, gardens and plants are often close to the windows.
