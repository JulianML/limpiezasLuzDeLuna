We restore glass that seems impossible to clean

A premises that has been closed or under renovation poses challenges that a standard clean cannot solve. We take care of:

Construction residue removal: We safely lift cement, plaster, paint and façade splashes without scratching the glass.

Vinyl & adhesive removal: We strip the previous tenant's advertising and adhesive residue baked on by the Benidorm sun.

Limescale & salt removal: If the premises has been closed for a while, the glass may be "burned" by salt. We use professional restoration products.
