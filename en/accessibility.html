Accessibility

ADRIÁN FRANCISCO ROMERO FERNÁNDEZ is committed to adapting the content of the website https://limpiezasluzdeluna.es/ so that everyone, regardless of disability, age, or whether they access the Web from less-conventional technologies, can have universal access to all the contents of this website, without encountering accessibility difficulties.

To this end, the accessibility criteria involve compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 1.0, at double-A level, established by the W3C (World Wide Web Consortium), an international association made up of different organisations, public and private, that work together to develop standards to promote and disseminate good practices in Web development.

We are also adopting current Spanish regulations in this area: Standard UNE 139803:2004 "Computer applications for people with disabilities: Accessibility requirements for Web content".

Compliance status

This website is partially compliant with Royal Decree 1112/2018, of 7 September, on the accessibility of websites and applications for mobile devices of the public sector, due to the lack of conformity of the aspects indicated below.

Non-accessible content

Despite efforts to keep the website accessible, some accessibility difficulties may be encountered at certain times for technical or maintenance reasons. However, periodic reviews are carried out to detect and correct possible accessibility issues.

The content of this website is designed to be accessible and is continually reviewed. However, and despite efforts to improve accessibility, there may be content that is not yet fully accessible, such as:

  • Occasional editing errors on a web page.
  • Disproportionate burden does not apply to this website.
  • Content outside the scope of applicable legislation, such as files in PDF format, third-party content not directly under the control of this website.

Optional content

The visible version of this accessibility policy for this website is that of 6 February 2025. Since then, daily reviews of the website's content are carried out to ensure its accessibility.

This accessibility statement applies to the website https://limpiezasluzdeluna.es/

The website applies the accessibility criteria of Standard UNE-EN 301549:2019, which regulates accessibility requirements for ICT products and services.

Among the measures adopted to improve accessibility are:

  • Use of alternative text in images.
  • Links with clear and concise descriptions.
  • Website structure based on XHTML 1.0 and CSS 3.0, with valid code according to W3C standards.
  • Responsive design that allows correct display on different devices and screen sizes.
  • Compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) at AA level.
  • Allows changing the text size and colours of the page through browser configuration options.
